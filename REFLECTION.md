# Start / Stop / Continue Reflection — Draft to personalize

This is a draft, not a claim about work already completed. Replace these prompts
with your own experience before submitting; aim for half a page to one page.

## START

One difficulty I actually encountered was [describe a concrete setup, test,
rounding, or debugging issue]. I will start [specific habit] because it would
have helped me [explain why]. On my next assignment, I will apply it by
[describe an action and when you will do it].

## STOP

A shortcut that backfired was [describe something you actually did]. Its effect
was [concrete consequence]. I will stop this pattern by [specific safeguard,
such as writing a behavior test before each calculation]. When I notice
[warning sign], I will [action that helps you change the habit].

## CONTINUE

A practice that helped me was [something you genuinely used successfully]. For
example, [describe one observed outcome]. I will continue it because [why it
worked and how it transfers to future assignments].

## Assistance

Describe honestly how you used assistance and which parts you personally
reviewed, changed, executed, and understood, as required by your course.
